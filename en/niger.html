Procedure

Cotonou → Niamey transit, step by step

Used vehicles and full containers alike: a fully mapped circuit, compliant with Benin's customs code.

1

Reception at the Port of Cotonou

We take over your B/L, verify documents and prepare the file before the vessel even berths.

2

T1 transit declaration + guarantee

International transit declaration and guarantee covering the crossing of Benin with duties suspended.

3

Regrouping & customs escort

Vehicles directed to the northern regrouping park, containers loaded on licensed trucks: departure in convoy under customs escort.

4

Malanville / Gaya border crossing

Transit discharge on the Benin side and handover to your correspondent or our partner for entry formalities into Niger.

5

Clearing & delivery in Niamey

Final clearing under Nigerien taxation and last-mile delivery, with a full report and supporting documents.

Taxation on the Niger side (indicative): ECOWAS CET duty of 20% or 35% depending on the category, 19% VAT, and a possible surtax for vehicles over 8 years old — most often 35% to 60% of the customs value in total. Exact rates depend on the HS code and regime: ask us for an estimate on your file.

FAQ

Transit to Niger: your questions

How long does Cotonou → Niamey transit take?
Allow 2 to 4 working days for formalities at the Port of Cotonou (T1 transit declaration, guarantee, registration), then road haulage under customs escort to the Malanville border, depending on convoy schedules. We share a precise timeline as soon as we receive your documents.
Are duties paid in Benin or in Niger?
Under international transit, goods cross Benin with duties suspended: a guarantee covers the journey and final clearing happens in Niger under Nigerien taxation (ECOWAS CET, 19% VAT). Our trusted correspondents in Niamey handle that last step.
Can you transit used vehicles to Niger?
Yes. Vehicles bound for Niger are registered at the dedicated transit facility, grouped at the northern regrouping park and convoyed under customs escort to the border. We manage the entire file and hand over to your correspondent.
What documents are required for transit to Niger?
The T1 transit declaration, the transit guarantee, the Bill of Lading or air waybill, the commercial invoice and the certificate of origin. We check your file before the vessel arrives to avoid any blockage or demurrage.
