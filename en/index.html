FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does customs clearing cost in Benin?
For used vehicles, total duties and taxes typically range from 35% to 55% of the CIF value depending on the type and engine. For general cargo, the ECOWAS Common External Tariff applies (0 to 35% duty plus 18% VAT). Send us your invoice or B/L and we will give you an exact figure.
Which documents do I need to import through Cotonou?
The essentials are the Bill of Lading, the commercial invoice, the certificate of origin and a Benin tax ID (IFU) — which we can arrange through our structure if you do not have a local entity. We check your file before the vessel arrives to avoid storage charges.
How long does clearing take at the Port of Cotonou?
On average 3 to 5 working days for a vehicle and 5 to 7 days for a container of general cargo, provided the documentation is complete on arrival. You can follow every step in our online tracking space.
Can you deliver my cargo to Niger or Nigeria?
Yes — this is our specialty. We run the Cotonou → Niamey transit corridor (T1 declaration, customs escort to the Malanville–Gaya border) and the Cotonou ⇄ Lagos corridor through Sèmè-Kraké, including regulated goods. One contract covers the whole journey.
