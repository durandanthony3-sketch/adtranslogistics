How it works

4 phases, 13 steps, no phone calls

  1. Documents Document exchange, delivery order, provisional destuffing list, transfer request, customs declaration.
  2. Approvals Port, Bénin Manutention, Bénin Terminal and BESC invoices — processed in parallel to save days.
  3. Release Release authorised, then positioning request for the container.
  4. Destuffing Destuffing sheets, deconsolidation and handover of the goods.
